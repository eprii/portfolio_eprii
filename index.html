The person, not the résumé.

02 Internship

BullCare

BullCare is a digital application designed to support the management and care of bulls at the Artificial Insemination Centre (Balai Inseminasi Buatan) in South Kalimantan. It digitises records of various maintenance activities, including feeding, health checks, sanitation, weighing, measuring, treatment, and semen collection.

  • Flutter
  • Firebase

03 Internship

SIAP

SIAP (Sistem Informasi-Administrasi Pegawai / Employee Administration Information System) is a mobile/web application developed to support employee administration at the Department of Plantation and Livestock Service in South Kalimantan. It helps employees manage and update personnel data and documents digitally in one integrated system.

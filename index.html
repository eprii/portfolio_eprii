02 / About

The person, not the résumé.

A software engineering student at SMK Telkom in Banjarbaru, Indonesia. I think of myself simply as a programmer. Not an expert, not a beginner. I'm someone who likes figuring things out.

Web, mobile, operating systems like Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, hardware, the occasional strange experiment. The interest isn't really “I want to be a developer.” It's closer to: how does this actually work?

Most people look at something and think, that's cool. I love using this.
I think: that's cool. How do I make this?
